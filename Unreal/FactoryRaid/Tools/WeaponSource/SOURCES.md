# Weapon and cloth source notes

- The five MK18-style meshes are original game art authored by `generate_mk18.py`. Visual reference: [Daniel Defense MK18](https://danieldefense.com/mk18), particularly the compact silhouette, RIS II quad rail and collapsible stock. No manufacturer model or logo is included.
- Fabric diffuse, roughness and DirectX normal maps: [Rough Linen, Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen), downloaded 2026-09-09. [CC0 asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license). The scan is used as textile microdetail beneath authored uniform colours and camouflage masks.
- The mannequin animations, functional weapon rig and projectiles remain Epic template assets. The replacement geometry retains the rig's muzzle position; it is visual game geometry and not a physical product design.
