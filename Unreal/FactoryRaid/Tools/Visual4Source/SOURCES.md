# Visual4 provenance

FAST-style shell, side rails, open front shroud, loop fields and retention straps are original game-art geometry generated by `../generate_fast.py`. They are visual approximations, not a licensed Ops-Core asset or manufacturing model.

Public visual references: manufacturer https://shop.gentexcorp.com/content/UM-1034.pdf and https://shop.gentexcorp.com/ops-core-railink-enabled-fast-sf-helmet-system/ . No images, trademarks, or mesh files from those pages were copied into the game.

Weapon and helmet icons are rendered from this project's actual meshes by `../render_loot_icons.py`.

Cloth materials retain the existing CC0 Poly Haven rough-linen scan (https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen), with finer tiling and lower normal intensity. Weapon and helmet surface details are procedural shaders created in `../prepare_visual4.py`. No paid asset was purchased.
